# TestFlight: What to test

Paste this into the build's "What to Test" in App Store Connect.

---

Controller Bridge 1.0 plays Syndicate from a Denon LC6000 PRIME. Please try:

- Setup: follow the setup guide on first launch. FEEDBACK should read LIVE.
- Play a set: transport, hot cues, loops, jog, the pitch fader and the Needle Drop strip.
- Four decks: Shift + deck select through layers 1-4. The ring and LEDs should follow each deck.
- LEDs: pads in your cue colors, and the ring in each deck's color.
- Recovery: unplug and replug the controller, sleep the Mac, and restart Syndicate. Everything should come back.
- If the LEDs stay dark, read the panel's "Why no LEDs?" help. Does it tell you what to fix?

Tell us about anything that didn't respond, lit the wrong color, or needed a relaunch.
