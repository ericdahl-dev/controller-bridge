Denon DJ LC6000 PRIME

DeckBridge for the LC6000 PRIME

Play Untitled Audio Syndicate from an LC6000 PRIME on your Mac, with the jog wheel and pitch fader at full 14-bit resolution and the controller lit from Syndicate’s own state.

Join the TestFlight beta Free · macOS 26 or later

What it fixes on the LC6000

Syndicate’s own MIDI mapping (0.3.7) can’t read the LC6000’s 14-bit jog wheel or pitch fader, and it gives LED feedback only to the DDJ-FLX4 and FLX10. DeckBridge reads the LC6000’s USB MIDI, sends it to Syndicate’s OSC input and lights the controller from Syndicate’s OSC feedback.

Lights that follow Syndicate

  • Play, Cue, Sync and Master follow Syndicate’s state. Play blinks while a quantized start is pending.
  • Pads show your hot cues in Syndicate’s cue colors. A held pad lights brighter.
  • The platter ring lights in its deck’s color when a track is loaded, and can pulse on each beat or on the downbeat.
  • Key Lock, Slip, Shift and the pad-mode buttons show DeckBridge’s own state.

Four decks from one LC6000

Shift + deck select steps through the LC6000’s four layers, and layer N drives Syndicate deck N. Anything held on the old layer is released, and the lights repaint from the new deck.

The pitch fader is shared by all four layers, so on a new layer it waits until it meets that deck’s tempo before it moves it.

What it can’t do yet

  • Scratching: Syndicate has no OSC route for scratch or scrub.
  • The platter’s center display: it needs messages Denon doesn’t document. Art on it is planned for v1.1.
  • Slicer mode: not mapped.
  • Pitch range (Shift + Pitch Bend): Syndicate has no OSC route for it.

Set up in four steps

The full setup guide

  1. Settings → OSC

    Turn OSC INPUT ENABLED on (port 9090).

  2. Settings → OSC Feedback

    Send feedback to 127.0.0.1, port 9096.

  3. Subscriptions

    Turn on the feedback subscriptions the guide lists.

  4. Settings → MIDI

    Turn the LC6000 PRIME’s MIDI input off in Syndicate. Quit any other DJ app using it first, and power-cycle the controller.
