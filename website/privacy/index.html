Privacy

DeckBridge collects no data

No account, no tracking, no analytics, no ads.

What DeckBridge collects

Nothing. DeckBridge doesn’t collect, store or send any personal information or usage data, and it contains no analytics or tracking code. Nothing leaves your Mac except the OSC messages described below.

Network use

DeckBridge sends OSC messages to the Syndicate DJ app and listens for its feedback. By default that’s on this Mac only (127.0.0.1). If you set it up for Syndicate on another Mac, it uses your local network, and macOS asks for your permission first. It talks to no other server.

What stays on your Mac

Your settings (deck, devices, ports and colors) and any mapping file you import are kept on your Mac, in the app’s own sandboxed storage. Deleting the app removes them.

This website

This site uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party fonts or scripts.
